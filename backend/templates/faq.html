Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tracking your farm work shares and managing your volunteer commitments.

How do I track my CSA work commitment?

Tracking your CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) work commitment is simple with HelpFarmers. Once your Farm Manager creates your account, simply log in, select the date of your shift, choose the crop you worked on, and enter your hours. The system automatically calculates your progress toward your seasonal work share goal.

What activities count toward my farm volunteer hours?

This depends on your specific farm's policies, but generally, we track core agricultural activities including Planting, Tending, Harvesting, Cultivating (weeding), moving dirt, and general off-season maintenance. You can specify your exact activity when logging your shift.

Can I log multiple work shifts on the same day?

Yes! If you spent the morning harvesting tomatoes and the afternoon planting peppers, you can log those as two separate entries. Your personal dashboard will automatically aggregate all your hours for the day.

Is HelpFarmers free for volunteers to use?

Absolutely. HelpFarmers is completely free for individual volunteers and CSA members. Farm Managers handle the subscription to access the administrative and analytics tools, ensuring the platform remains ad-free and secure for everyone in the field.
