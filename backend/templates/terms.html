3. Electronic Signatures (E-SIGN Act)

By typing your name to sign a compliance document or waiver on this platform, you consent to the use of electronic signatures. You agree that your electronic signature is legally binding and carries the same weight as a physical, handwritten signature under the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce (E-SIGN) Act. Signed documents are stored in an immutable WORM (Write Once, Read Many) vault to preserve audit integrity.

4. Farm Subscriptions & Billing

Farm Managers who register a farm are responsible for maintaining an active subscription.

  • 60-Day Trial: New farms receive a 60-day free trial with full feature access. No credit card is required to begin.
  • Read-Only State: If a trial expires or a subscription payment fails, the farm workspace will be downgraded to a "Read-Only" state. Historical data is preserved, but no new volunteers may join, and no new hours may be logged until the account is brought into good standing.
  • Refunds: Subscriptions are billed annually. We do not offer prorated refunds for mid-season cancellations.
