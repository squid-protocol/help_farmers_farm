Honoring the hands that feed our communities.

Helping Farmers Farm started in the dirt—built by a CSA volunteer and a local farmer to solve a simple problem: managing work shares shouldn't be harder than growing the food.

Our Story

I'm a CSA volunteer who noticed my farmer spending way too much time wrestling with clipboards, spreadsheets, and lost liability waivers instead of tending to the crops. We decided there had to be a better way, so we built this platform together.

We aim to truly help farmers farm. We do this by bringing passionate volunteers to your farm, honoring their hard work with personal impact dashboards, and making roster onboarding and legal waiver compliance completely automated. Our goal is simple: let you focus on the fields, and not worry about the paperwork.
