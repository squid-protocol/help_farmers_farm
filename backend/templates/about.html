Honoring the hands that feed our communities.

Helping Farmers Farm was built to solve a simple problem: managing CSA work shares shouldn't be harder than growing the actual food.

Our Mission

Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) and cooperative farms are the backbone of sustainable local food systems. But tracking the hundreds of volunteer hours required to keep them running is often a chaotic mess of clipboards and spreadsheets.

We believe that logging hours shouldn't just be an administrative chore. It should be a celebration. Helping Farmers Farm turns shift tracking into a gamified, visual experience, allowing volunteers to see their exact impact while giving Farm Managers the clean, multi-tenant analytics they need to run their business.
