Honoring the hands that feed our communities.

Helping Farmers Farm started in the dirt—built by a CSA volunteer and a local farmer to solve a simple problem: managing work shares shouldn't be harder than growing the food.

Our Story

I'm a CSA volunteer who noticed my farmer spending way too much time wrestling with clipboards, spreadsheets, and lost liability waivers instead of tending to the crops. We built this platform to fix that, but quickly realized the problem was much bigger: passionate people want to get their hands in the dirt, but they don't know where to go.

We transformed into a discovery network. For Volunteers, we are a free portal to find local agriculture work share programs where volunteers commit to working on a farm for a few hours per week for a season, and track lifetime impact. For Farms, we are help honor your volunteers by making sure they know their impact to your harvest, we also can track seasonal logs so you know what your labor force did over the season and we have a liability waiver compliance tool that automates legal waivers.
